# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 30, 2026**

Kumpu App ("we", "us", or "our") operates the KangMeas FM mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII). The app is designed as a radio streaming client and does not require user registration or account creation.

### Data we do NOT collect:
*   Name, email address, or phone number.
*   Location data.
*   Contacts or files from your device.

## 2. Usage Data and Internet Access
The app requires internet access to:
*   Stream audio from radio station servers.
*   Fetch station logos and metadata.
*   Provide Chromecast support (Google Cast).

Standard technical identifiers (such as IP address or device model) may be visible to the streaming servers you connect to, just as they would be when visiting a website in a browser. We do not store or track this data.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app uses Google Play Services for:
*   **Google Cast (Chromecast)**: To allow you to play audio on compatible devices.
Google may collect data as governed by their own privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Security
We value your trust. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data at risk of exposure from our app.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
